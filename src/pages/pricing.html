The fine print

How pricing works

Sized by your dog, not by the clock

Dog prices are set by size — small, medium, large or XL — because a Maltese and a Shepherd need very different amounts of bath, dryer time and coat work, even though both appointments run about 2 hours.

Coat condition matters

Matt removal is the one service priced by severity rather than a fixed rate, starting at CA$120. Heavy coat, deep matting or extra work is always discussed before it starts — the final price reflects the time it actually takes.

Deposits & payment

A $50 deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance is paid by e-Transfer when the groom is complete.
