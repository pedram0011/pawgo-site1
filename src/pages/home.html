Good to know

Questions pet parents ask first

Straight answers before you book — and a full FAQ for everything else.

Do you groom cats as well as dogs?

Yes. Cat appointments include baths, sanitary trims, lion cuts and nail trims — all done one-on-one and cage-free. See cat grooming.

How does mobile grooming work?

Susan brings the grooming table, tub and dryer to your address. Your pet is groomed on site, one pet at a time, and handed back to you at the door.

How much does a groom cost?

Dog full grooms run CA$110–CA$210 by size, bath & tidy starts at CA$85, and cat grooming starts at CA$100. See the full price list.

Which areas do you serve?

Vancouver, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Surrey, Langley, North and West Vancouver, Richmond, Delta, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows and White Rock. Full service area list.

How do I book and pay?

A {{deposit}} deposit secures the appointment and is applied toward the total cost, and the remaining balance is paid by e-Transfer after the groom. Go to the booking page.
