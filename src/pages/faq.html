Pawgo is a mobile grooming service. Susan brings the grooming table, tub and dryer to your address and grooms your pet on site, one pet at a time, cage-free. Your pet never has to travel or wait in a salon.

Timing, coats & comfort

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Full dog grooms take about 2 hours and cat grooms 1.5–2 hours depending on coat length. Quick add-ons are much shorter.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well on a 4–8 week rhythm, but coat type, lifestyle and shedding cycles all change the answer. Read the grooming frequency guide.

How often should my cat be groomed?

Short-haired cats often only need occasional professional sessions, while long-haired cats benefit from more regular coat work to prevent matting. See cat grooming options.

Cats

Cat grooming questions

Do you groom cats as well as dogs?

Yes. Cat appointments include bath + brush + blow dry from CA$100, bath + brush + sanitary trim from CA$120, lion cuts from CA$140 (with a bath from CA$160), nail trims CA$20 and de-matting {{demattingRate}} — all done one-on-one and cage-free. See cat grooming.

What is a lion cut?

A lion cut takes the body coat down short while leaving the head, mane, tail tip and booties. It's the standard reset for heavily coated or badly matted long-haired cats, available with or without a bath, and it takes about 2 hours.
