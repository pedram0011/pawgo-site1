Grooming services for dogs & cats, delivered to your door

01

Full groom packages

The complete spa day for dogs who need a haircut, not just a wash. Your full groom includes a haircut, bath, blow dry, nail trim and sanitary shave — all in a single calm, one-on-one visit at your address.

Who it's for: dogs whose coat is cut and styled rather than left at natural length, and anyone who wants the works in one appointment. Pricing is set by your dog's size, so a Toy Poodle and a Bernese don't pay the same rate.

Full grooms take around 2 hours regardless of size — the difference is the amount of coat to wash, dry and trim.

Undercoat removal / deshedding

Built for double-coated breeds and heavy shedders. This package focuses on clearing the loose undercoat rather than cutting the topcoat, and includes deshedding, bath, blow dry, nail trim and sanitary shave.

Who it's for: dogs who leave clumps on the couch, blow their coat twice a year, or are approaching a shed and need the loose hair managed before it ends up on your furniture.

Small dogs are in and out in 1.5 hours; medium, large and XL dogs take 2 hours — there's simply more undercoat to work through.

Feline friends (cat grooming)

Gentle, patient grooming tailored for cats — in a quiet, familiar setting with no dogs barking in the next room. Your cat is the only pet on the table.

Who it's for: long-haired cats who mat easily, cats who need a bath or tidy-up, and owners who'd rather not subject their cat to a busy salon. Sessions are priced by coat length, and a lion cut is available for heavily coated cats.

Short-hair cats take 1.5 hours; long-hair cats and lion cuts take 2 hours.

Mat removal only

A focused appointment for pets with localized matting who don't need a full bath or style. Matting is worked out carefully over roughly a 1-hour visit — no bath, no styling.

Who it's for: pets with tight patches behind the ears, under the armpits or around the hindquarters. The price depends on how severe the matting is and how long it takes to remove comfortably, which is why this service starts at a published minimum rather than a fixed rate.

Because the price varies by severity, matting is assessed at the appointment — the final cost reflects how long it takes to work out comfortably.

Nail trims

Before you book

One deposit, one clear price

Please note: a $50 deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance can be paid via e-Transfer upon completion of the groom.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Nail trims take 15–30 minutes, mat removal about an hour, cat grooms 1.5–2 hours, and dog grooms 1.5–2 hours depending on size and package.

Do you charge extra for matted coats?

Mat removal is priced from CA$120 and varies with severity. Full grooms are priced by your dog's size — the price list shows both.

Can I add a nail trim to any package?

Nail trims are already included in full groom and deshedding packages. Standalone nail trims are available for quick visits between full grooms.
