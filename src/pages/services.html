Grooming services for dogs & cats, delivered to your door

Every Pawgo appointment is one pet, one groomer, no cages. Choose a service below — prices are published as starting prices, and the balance is paid only when the groom is done.

01

Dog full groom

The complete spa day for dogs who need a haircut, not just a wash. Your full groom includes bath with shampoo & conditioner, blow dry, brushing, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, paw pad trim, and sanitary trim — all in a single calm, one-on-one visit at your address.

Who it's for: dogs whose coat is cut and styled rather than left at natural length, and anyone who wants the works in one appointment. Pricing is set by your dog's size — small, medium, large, XL or XXL.

A bath, blow dry and tidy-up without a full haircut. Includes bath, shampoo & conditioner, blow dry, brushing, nail trim, ear cleaning, paw pads, sanitary trim, and light face/feet tidy as needed.

Who it's for: dogs who need a thorough clean, nail and ear care and a neat tidy — but not a full haircut. Priced by your dog's size, small through XXL.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Dog grooms take around 2 hours and cat grooms 1.5–2 hours depending on size, coat and service chosen. Quick add-ons are much shorter.

Do you charge extra for matted coats?

{{demattingPolicy}} All prices are starting prices, and any additional charges are discussed with you before they are applied.

Can I add a nail trim to any package?

Nail trims are included in dog full grooms and bath & tidy services. A dog nail trim can also be added to any appointment for CA$20, and cats have a CA$20 nail trim.
