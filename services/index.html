Before you book

One deposit, one clear price

Please note: a $30 deposit is required to secure your booking and is applied toward the total cost of the grooming service. The remaining balance can be paid via e-Transfer upon completion of the groom.

All prices are starting prices and may vary depending on your pet's breed, size, coat condition, temperament, and the time required for grooming. Any additional charges will always be discussed with you before they are applied.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Dog grooms take around 2 hours and cat grooms 1.5–2 hours depending on size, coat and service chosen. Quick add-ons are much shorter.

Do you charge extra for matted coats?

De-matting is charged by time at CA$20 per 15 minutes, depending on the severity of the matting and the actual time required — 15 minutes is CA$20, 30 minutes is CA$40, 45 minutes is CA$60 and 60 minutes is CA$80. All prices are starting prices, and any additional charges are discussed with you before they are applied.

Can I add a nail trim to any package?

Nail trims are included in dog full grooms and bath & tidy services. A dog nail trim can also be added to any appointment for CA$20, and cats have a CA$20 nail trim.
