Booking & payment

Getting an appointment

How does mobile grooming work?

Pawgo is a mobile grooming service. Susan brings the grooming table, tub and dryer to your address and grooms your pet on site, one pet at a time, cage-free. Your pet never has to travel or wait in a salon.

How do I book an appointment?

Start on the booking page — it lists the current way to book. A $50 deposit secures the appointment, and the remaining balance is paid by e-Transfer after the groom. Go to booking.

How do I pay?

A $50 deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance can be paid via e-Transfer upon completion of the groom.

During the groom

Timing, coats & comfort

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Nail trims take 15–30 minutes, mat removal about 1 hour, dog deshedding 1.5–2 hours, full dog grooms about 2 hours, and cat grooms 1.5–2 hours depending on coat length.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well on a 4–8 week rhythm, but coat type, lifestyle and shedding cycles all change the answer. Read the grooming frequency guide.

How often should my cat be groomed?

Short-haired cats often only need occasional professional sessions, while long-haired cats benefit from more regular coat work to prevent matting. See cat grooming options.

Do you groom nervous pets?

The mobile, one-on-one setup is designed for pets who find salons stressful: no car ride, no cage, no other animals, and Susan's full attention for the entire appointment.

Do you handle matted coats?

Yes. Mat removal is available as a standalone service from CA$120 — about 1 hour, no bath or styling, with the final price depending on severity. Mat removal details.

Cats

Cat grooming questions

Do you groom cats as well as dogs?

Yes. Cat appointments include short-hair grooms (1.5 hrs, CA$150), long-hair grooms (2 hrs, CA$170) and lion cuts (2 hrs, CA$190) — all done one-on-one and cage-free. See cat grooming.

What is a lion cut?

A lion cut takes the body coat down short while leaving the head, mane, tail tip and booties. It's the standard reset for heavily coated or badly matted long-haired cats, and it takes about 2 hours.
