Dog grooming

Dog grooming in Greater Vancouver — minus the car ride and the cage

Pawgo is a mobile dog grooming service: Susan brings the table, tub and dryer to your address and grooms one dog at a time, cage-free, from Vancouver to White Rock.

Bath & tidy from CA$85 $30 deposit to book 15 cities served

Most dogs don't love the salon. The car ride, the crate, the barking, the waiting — by the time they get home, they're exhausted before the bath even starts.

Pawgo works differently. The grooming comes to you. Susan parks at your address and grooms your dog on site, one-on-one, with no other animals around and no crate to wait in. It's the same full salon experience — bath, blow dry, haircut, nails — in a calmer setting, and you don't lose half a day to drop-off and pick-up.

Dogs are groomed one at a time. That means your dog gets the whole appointment, start to finish, and Susan gets to notice things a busy salon might miss — a new lump, a sore paw, an ear that's bothering them.

Which service?

Choosing by coat, not by breed

You don't need to know your dog's breed standard — just how their coat behaves.

Pick the full groom if…

Your dog's coat gets cut. Poodles, doodles, Bichon types, Schnauzers and most terriers need length taken off to keep their shape. The full groom covers the cut, the bath, the dry, the nails and the sanitary trim in one visit.

Pick the bath & tidy if…

Your dog's coat stays at natural length and doesn't need a haircut — a thorough bath, blow dry, nail trim, ear cleaning and a light face/feet tidy is enough. Double-coated dogs who just need the loose hair managed can add a de-shedding treatment.

Not sure? Every option is listed with everything it includes on the services page, and all starting prices sit together on the pricing page.

The process

What happens during a Pawgo dog groom

  1. Susan arrives at your address

    Your appointment time is yours — no drop-off, no waiting room. Your dog stays with you until it's time.

  2. Bath & blow dry

    A proper wash and a thorough dry, done one dog at a time so nothing is rushed.

  3. Cut, tidy & nails

    Haircut, paw pad and sanitary trim for full grooms, or bath and light tidying for bath & tidy — plus nail trim and ear cleaning.

  4. Handover at your door

    Your dog comes back to you fresh and relaxed. Pay the balance by e-Transfer when the groom is done.

Dog grooming FAQ

Common questions from dog owners

How long does a dog groom take?

Full grooms take about 2 hours for every size.

How much does dog grooming cost in Greater Vancouver?

At Pawgo, dog full grooms start at CA$110 (small), CA$130 (medium), CA$155 (large), CA$185 (XL) and CA$210 (XXL). Bath & tidy starts at CA$85, and doodle & poodle full grooms start at CA$125. All prices are starting prices. See the full price list.

Do you groom nervous or anxious dogs?

Anxious, nervous, reactive, and difficult-to-groom dogs are welcome. Dogs with a history of aggression may also be accepted on a case-by-case basis, as long as grooming can be performed safely — safety is always the deciding factor. Please tell us about any history of aggression or biting before booking. The mobile setup helps too: no car ride, no cage, no other dogs, and one groomer's full attention for the whole appointment.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

De-matting is charged by time at CA$20 per 15 minutes, depending on the severity of the matting and the actual time required — 15 minutes is CA$20, 30 minutes is CA$40, 45 minutes is CA$60 and 60 minutes is CA$80. Heavy matting inside a full groom is assessed at the appointment, with any additional charges discussed with you first. Add-on details.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well on a 4–8 week rhythm, but it depends entirely on coat type and lifestyle. Read the full grooming frequency guide.

Before your visit

Prepare in five minutes

A little prep makes the groom smoother and shorter for everyone.

  • Brush out tangles the day before — matting tightens after a bath.
  • Let Susan know about health issues, sensitivities or recent procedures.
  • Clear a parking spot and a little space near the door for the mobile setup.
