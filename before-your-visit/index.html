Before your visit

How to prepare your pet for a mobile groom

A little preparation makes the appointment shorter, calmer and kinder for your pet. Here's exactly what to do before Susan arrives, what to have ready, and what happens on the day.

Takes 5 minutes Dogs & cats

Step by step

From booking to handover

  1. Book & deposit

    Reserve your slot online and secure it with a $50 deposit. The balance is paid after the groom.

  2. Prepare the day before

    Brush out tangles, note anything Susan should know, and make space near your door.

  3. The groom

    Susan arrives at your address and grooms your pet one-on-one, cage-free, for the booked duration.

  4. Handover & payment

    Your pet comes back to you at your own door. Pay the remaining balance by e-Transfer.

The day before

What to do before the appointment

  • Brush your pet the day before. Mats tighten after a bath, so tangles are much easier to work out while the coat is dry.
  • Leave the coat as it is. Don't attempt a DIY trim or cut out mats with scissors the day before — skin folds into mats and cuts easily.
  • Make a little space. Clear a parking spot and some room near your door so the mobile setup can work comfortably.
  • Keep your pet reachable. On grooming day, keep dogs in the yard or house and cats indoors so nobody has to be chased at appointment time.
  • Note anything medical. Recent procedures, skin conditions, medications, phobias or past grooming reactions — all useful to mention when you book.

What to bring

Not much, honestly — the whole point of mobile grooming is that you don't have to pack a bag. Useful things to have on hand:

  • Your pet's usual brush or comb, if you use one
  • Treats your pet likes, for calm reinforcement afterwards
  • A list of any medications or health notes worth flagging

No special paperwork is needed to book — but if your pet has a health condition, mention it when you book so Susan can factor it in.

On the day

Preparing dogs

  • A short walk before the appointment helps burn off restless energy.
  • Feed as normal but avoid a big meal right before the groom — a full stomach plus a bath isn't comfortable.
  • Have your dog on a collar or harness ready for the handover at the door.
  • Tell Susan upfront if your dog is anxious, sound-sensitive or protective of paws and ears.

Preparing cats

  • Keep cats indoors on grooming day so they're easy to find.
  • Leave them in a familiar room rather than shoving them in a carrier to wait.
  • A calm, quiet morning sets the tone — skip rough play-fights just before the appointment.
  • Share any past reactions to grooming, brushes or nail handling.

During the appointment

The setup arrives at your address and your pet is groomed on site, one-on-one. Expect the booked duration: 15–30 minutes for nail trims, around an hour for mat removal, 1.5–2 hours for cat grooms and 1.5–2 hours for dog grooms depending on package and size.

Susan works through bath, dry, cut or deshed, nails and tidy-up as covered by your booking — with nothing else on the schedule to rush the session.

Handover

Pickup & payment

There's no pickup run to schedule: your pet is handed back to you at your own door when the groom is finished. Pay the remaining balance by e-Transfer at that point — the $50 deposit you paid when booking is already accounted for.

Take a moment to check the finish — the cut, the nails, the sanitary trim — while Susan is still there, so anything can be sorted out immediately rather than by email later.

Afterwards

Aftercare basics

  • Give your pet a quiet, warm spot to settle — post-groom naps are normal.
  • Offer water; some pets are extra thirsty after a bath and dryer.
  • Run your hands over the skin: if you feel any remaining small tangles, gently work them out with a comb before they tighten.
  • Start the brushing rhythm again within a few days — see the coat care between grooms guide.
  • Book the next appointment before the coat catches up; 4–8 weeks works for most dogs (frequency guide).

Quick answers

Last-minute questions

What if my pet is matted and I didn't brush beforehand?

That's fine — mat removal is a standard service from CA$120, priced by severity. Just don't attempt to cut mats out yourself first.

What time should I be ready?

Have your pet accessible and your space ready at the booked appointment time — the full groom duration starts once the session begins.
