About

Hi, I'm Susan — the (only) groomer at Pawgo

Pawgo Mobile Grooming is a one-groomer service. When you book, you're booking me, and when I arrive, your pet is the only pet I'm looking after that day.

Susan with a freshly groomed Pomeranian — Greater Vancouver.

Why Pawgo exists

Grooming the way I believe it should be done

I believe every pet deserves to be treated with patience, love and the utmost care. That's not a slogan I came up with for a website — it's the reason Pawgo works the way it does.

Most grooming happens in a room full of crates, dryers and other people's dogs. Your pet waits its turn, gets worked on between two other appointments, and goes home stressed. I wanted the opposite: one pet, one groomer, no cage, no noise, and all the time the coat actually needs.

So I put the salon on wheels. I bring the table, the tub and the dryer to your driveway in Greater Vancouver, and your dog or cat gets a full, cage-free, one-on-one session without ever leaving home. You get your afternoon back. They get a calm groom.

What I care about

Three things I won't compromise on

Patience over speed

Some pets need a break mid-groom. Some take ten minutes to settle. The appointment is yours — there's no second dog waiting in the wings to rush us along.

One-on-one, always

Cage-free isn't a room with bigger cages. Your pet is with me for the whole appointment — no crates, no waiting in a kennel, no other animals.

Honest prices

Every price is published on this website — small dog to XL, short-hair cat to lion cut. You'll never finish a groom and find a number you didn't expect.

A day with Pawgo

What working with me looks like

A $50 deposit secures the appointment. I arrive at the time we agreed, set up at your address, and your pet is groomed from start to finish by me — bath, dry, cut or deshed, nails, the lot.

When we're done, your pet comes straight back to you at your own door. The remaining balance is paid by e-Transfer after the groom, and that's it.

I keep my prices, my service areas and my policies on this website in plain language, because I'd rather you know everything before you book than be surprised afterwards.
